# KDP printing cost calculator: cost and royalty

> Printing cost sets the floor on your list price. Formula: fixed cost + (page count × cost per page). Royalty shown uses the 60% paperback rate minus printing cost.

Canonical HTML: https://coverfit.app/printing-cost-calculator

## Rates used on the page

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed cost | $0.85 |
| Black ink | $0.012 per page |
| Standard color | $0.0225 per page |
| Premium color | $0.0575 per page |
| Royalty | 60% minus printing cost |

Rates last reviewed in 2024. KDP can change them; the calculator's fields are editable so you can match your account.

## Worked example

At 320 pages and $0.012 per page: $0.85 + (320 × $0.012) = $4.69. At a $14.99 list price the royalty is $8.99 − $4.69 = $4.30 per copy. KDP rounds in its own way, so treat the result as an estimate.

## Printing cost by page count

| Pages | Black ink | Standard color | Premium color |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | $2.05 | $3.10 | $6.60 |
| 200 | $3.25 | $5.35 | $12.35 |
| 320 | $4.69 | $8.05 | $19.25 |
| 400 | $5.65 | $9.85 | $23.85 |
| 500 | $6.85 | $12.10 | $29.60 |

Premium color is the trap for illustrated books: at 320 pages the printing cost alone is $19.25, so a $24.99 list price leaves almost nothing. Most illustrated paperbacks keep the interior black ink and put color only on the cover.

## What this calculator does not include

- Taxes, currency conversion, or marketplace-specific pricing.
- Expanded distribution (40% royalty) and hardcover rates.
- Promotional discounts, which reduce the royalty base.
- Large-print or non-standard trim surcharges.
