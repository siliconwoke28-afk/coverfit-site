About

Why CoverFit exists

Most cover tools either upload your artwork or bury the one number that matters: the spine width. CoverFit does neither. It runs in the browser, computes the wrap from your interior, and exports the file.

What it does

CoverFit builds a full-wrap paperback cover from three inputs: trim size, page count, and paper stock. It renders the back cover, spine, and front cover on one canvas, shows bleed and safe-area guides, and exports a 300 DPI PNG or a single-page PDF at the exact inch dimensions.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload, store, or process your image on a server. Files stay in the browser.
  • It does not replace KDP's previewer. Every file still needs to be checked there.
  • It does not provide licensed fonts or stock art. You bring assets you have the right to use.
  • It is not affiliated with Amazon or Kindle Direct Publishing.

Who it is for

Self-publishers who already have an interior and need a compliant wrap without learning a desktop layout program. The tool is deliberately narrow: it handles cover geometry well and leaves illustration, typography craft, and marketing to you.

Editorial standard

Dimensions and paper-thickness figures come from KDP's published paperback specifications. Pricing figures are labelled with the date they were last reviewed and are editable, because KDP changes rates. When a number cannot be verified, the page says so instead of presenting a guess as fact.
