# Contact CoverFit

> Email reaches us fastest. Include the trim size, page count, paper stock, and browser you used if the issue is about an export.

Canonical HTML: https://coverfit.app/contact

- Email: fit@coverfit.app
- Reply time: 1 to 3 business days
- Languages: English

## Useful details to include

- The exact trim size and page count you entered.
- Whether the paper was white or cream.
- Which export failed: PNG, PDF, or both.
- Browser and operating system.
- A screenshot of the preview, if the layout looks wrong.

## What we cannot do

We cannot check your KDP account, recover a manuscript, or guarantee that a file will be accepted by KDP. Those decisions belong to Amazon. We can check the geometry our tool produces against the published specs.

## Before you write

Most dimension questions are answered on the [KDP cover template](https://coverfit.app/kdp-cover-template) page or by the [spine width calculator](https://coverfit.app/spine-width-calculator). Export problems are usually a browser blocking downloads; try a second browser before reporting.
