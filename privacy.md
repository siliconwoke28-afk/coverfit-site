# Privacy policy

> Your images and cover text stay in your browser. There is no account, no upload endpoint, and no cover storage.

Canonical HTML: https://coverfit.app/privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

## What stays on your device

Cover images, titles, subtitles, author names, colors, and exported files are processed with the browser's canvas API. They are not transmitted to CoverFit. Closing the tab discards them.

## What may be collected

Like most websites, the hosting provider and analytics may record standard request data: IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp. This is used for security and aggregate traffic measurement, not to identify individual users.

If advertising is enabled in the future, advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technologies. Any such change will be reflected on this page before it takes effect.

## Third parties

- Cloudflare hosts the site and may process request logs and security data.
- Email sent to the contact address is handled by our email provider and forwarding destination.

## Your choices

You can block cookies or scripts in your browser without breaking the cover tool, because the tool itself does not depend on them. You can contact us to ask what data, if any, is associated with a request.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
